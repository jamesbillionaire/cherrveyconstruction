About CHERRVEY

Built from Mindanao. Built for bigger work.

CHERRVEY Construction Services began operations in 2021 under the leadership of Harvey John M. Pong. From its base in El Salvador City, Misamis Oriental, the firm executes construction, facility improvement, and technical infrastructure projects for government and institutional clients.

The practice sits at the intersection of building works and ICT infrastructure — structured cabling, data rooms, CCTV, and network rehabilitation — delivered with the documentation government and institutional clients require.
