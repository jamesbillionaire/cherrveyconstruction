Company

A Mindanao contractor built for construction and technical infrastructure.

CHERRVEY Construction Services began operations in 2021 under the leadership of Harvey John M. Pong. From Zone 12, Molugan, El Salvador City, Misamis Oriental, the firm delivers construction, facility improvement, and ICT infrastructure work for government and institutional clients.

Mission

CHERRVEY delivers construction and technical infrastructure work with disciplined workmanship, coordinated site-to-office execution, and cost-conscious scheduling. Professional personnel, skilled trade partners, and practical project monitoring keep the work visible, controllable, and complete.

Vision

To be among the region's most reliable construction and infrastructure partners — recognized for workmanship, documented quality, and commitment to its people — and to carry that standard nationwide by 2033.
